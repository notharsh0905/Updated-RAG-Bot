OFFICIAL UNIVERSITY NOTICE REGARDING CSJMU_SPECIAL_TEST_POLICY_1785517512.
The CSJMU Executive Board has enacted a new policy titled CSJMU_SPECIAL_TEST_POLICY_1785517512.
Under this policy, all UIET B.Tech students completing 8th semester with distinction will receive a special merit grant of Rs. 75,000 directly into their bank accounts.
Application procedure for CSJMU_SPECIAL_TEST_POLICY_1785517512 opens on September 15th every year via the student portal.
