Hostel Information


CSJM University, Kanpur provides six on-campus hostels with a total capacity of 1114 students, including two boys’ hostels (Shivaji – 400 seats and Swarn Jayanti – 200 seats) and four girls’ hostels (Ganga – 72 seats, Triveni – 108 seats, Saraswati – 218 seats, and Kaveri – 116 seats). Rooms are available in two-seater and three-seater options and are allotted by hostel wardens.


The hostels offer mess facilities with four meals daily (breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner), managed by an external service provider, with proper hygiene and a complaint system. Students must submit required documents such as undertakings and anti-ragging declarations during application.


Facilities include 24×7 electricity, security, RO drinking water, first-aid and ambulance services, newspapers, and housekeeping. Additional facilities like Wi-Fi, hot water, laundry, and library are proposed. Recreational options include indoor and outdoor sports, gym facilities, cultural activities, and student councils.

process of allotment of hostel
(a.) Completing the Admission process at corresponding Department campus of CSJM University Kanpur. (b.) Take Hardcopy Fees Receipt of Admission in corresponding department. (c.) See the Hostel Fees structure of Boys and Girls. (d.) Download application form from the web site of CSJM University Kanpur. For the point no 3 and 4 Use website csjmu.ac.in/Facilities /Hostels link (e.) After filling application form of Hostel, attach Admission Fees receipt of of the course of (department) in which admission taken and meet with Hostel Warden or Care Taker of the corresponding Hostel. (f.) Submit the Application form. (g.) Submit the annual Room Rent Fees of Hostel by the Demand Draft or  online in HDFC Bank. DD should be in the favors of Finance Officer CSJM University Kanpur. (h.) Temporary mess is going on please join it.


Hostel Curfew & Entry Rules:
- Girls Hostel Curfew: 7:30 PM entry timing (extended up to 8:00 PM with prior written permission from Hostel Warden).
- Boys Hostel Curfew: 9:30 PM entry timing.
- Outing Gate Pass: Required from Hostel Warden for overnight leave or off-campus stay.

Hostel fees and mess fees are applicable separately, and payments once made are generally non-refundable.
